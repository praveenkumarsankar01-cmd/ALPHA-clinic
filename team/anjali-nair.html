Background

About Dr. Anjali Nair

Dr. Nair completed her DNB in Obstetrics and Gynaecology followed by a fellowship in Reproductive Medicine, and has worked in assisted reproduction units in Chennai and Kochi. She has personally overseen more than two thousand stimulated cycles and around nine hundred IVF and ICSI cycles.

She is direct with couples about prognosis, and does not recommend IVF where a simpler pathway has a fair chance of working. Counselling runs alongside treatment as part of the package rather than as a billed extra, because the emotional cost of fertility treatment is, in her view, the part clinics most often ignore.

Fertility consultations are scheduled so that you are not waiting in a general area, and records are held separately.

Areas of expertise
  • Fertility assessment for both partners
  • Ovarian reserve and hormone profiling
  • Ovulation induction and follicular tracking
  • Intrauterine insemination (IUI)
  • IVF and ICSI cycle management
  • Recurrent pregnancy loss
  • Male factor infertility, in liaison with andrology
  • Fertility preservation counselling

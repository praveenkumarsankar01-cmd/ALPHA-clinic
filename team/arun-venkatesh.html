Background

About Dr. Arun Venkatesh

Dr. Venkatesh completed his MDS in Prosthodontics and Crown-Bridge and has placed over eight hundred implants since. He works to a simple rule: nothing is drilled before the patient has seen the digital X-ray on screen and has a written, itemised estimate in hand, with the urgent work separated from what can safely wait.

Because the dental chair sits in the same building as the physicians and the laboratory, patients on anticoagulants or with poorly controlled diabetes can be assessed medically before a procedure — a coordination most standalone dental practices cannot offer.

Emergency slots for severe dental pain are kept free every working day.

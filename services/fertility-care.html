Fertility Care

Assessment, IUI and IVF pathways — explained plainly, priced openly, and handled in confidence.

Overview

What is fertility care at Alpha Clinic?

Fertility care at Alpha Clinic begins with assessing both partners together, not just the woman. A great deal of unexplained infertility turns out to be explainable once hormone profiles, semen analysis and tubal patency have all been looked at properly.

We will tell you honestly what your chances are with each option, including the option of waiting. No one is pushed toward IVF because it is the most expensive line on the price list.

  • Assessment of both partners
  • Hormone and ovarian reserve profiling
  • Semen analysis in our own lab
  • Ovulation tracking and timed cycles
  • IUI and IVF pathways
  • Counselling included at every stage
What we offer

Fertility Care treatments

Fertility Assessment

A structured work-up for both partners: bloods, ultrasound, ovarian reserve and semen analysis, reviewed together in one consultation.

Ovulation Induction & IUI

Medicated cycles with follicular tracking, and intrauterine insemination where the tubes are open and sperm parameters allow.

IVF & ICSI Pathways

Full in-vitro cycles with embryo transfer, coordinated with our partner embryology unit and managed by the same consultant throughout.

What to expect

You will meet the same consultant every visit

Fertility treatment is long, and repeating your history to a new face each month is exhausting. One consultant owns your case from the first assessment through to a positive test, and the counselling that runs alongside it is included rather than billed as an extra.

  • One named consultant for the whole journey
  • Costs given per cycle, in writing, up front
  • Counselling built in, not charged separately
  • Honest guidance on when to stop or pause

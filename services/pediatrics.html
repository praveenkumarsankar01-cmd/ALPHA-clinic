Pediatrics

Newborn checks, the full immunisation schedule, and same-day appointments for a sick child.

Home/Services/Pediatrics
Overview

What is pediatrics at Alpha Clinic?

Our paediatric clinic runs every day, because a feverish child cannot wait for a Tuesday slot. We cover newborn examinations, the complete IAP immunisation schedule, growth and developmental review, and the ordinary run of childhood illness.

Consultations are unhurried and explained to parents in plain language, with written instructions on dosage and on the specific warning signs that should bring you back.

  • Complete IAP immunisation schedule
  • Growth and developmental milestone tracking
  • Same-day sick-child appointments
  • Newborn and preterm follow-up
  • Nutrition, feeding and sleep guidance
  • Written dosage and warning-sign instructions
What we offer

Pediatrics treatments

Vaccination

The full IAP schedule with digital reminders, plus catch-up plans for children whose vaccinations have fallen behind.

Growth & Development

Height, weight and head circumference plotted against standard charts, with milestone review and early referral where needed.

Childhood Illness

Fever, cough and wheeze, diarrhoea, ear and throat infections, allergies and asthma — assessed the same day you call.

What to expect

Vaccinations tracked so you do not have to

Missed doses are the most common gap we see. Every child on our register is on a digital immunisation schedule, and you get a reminder before each due date along with a card you can carry to school admissions.

  • Digital schedule with reminders before each due date
  • Catch-up plans for delayed vaccinations
  • Printed card accepted for school admission
  • Cold-chain maintained and audited

Maternity Care

One obstetric team from your first scan through to your six-week postnatal check.

Home/Services/Maternity Care
Overview

What is maternity care at Alpha Clinic?

Our maternity service covers the whole arc of a pregnancy: confirmation and dating, the anomaly and growth scans, nutrition and anaemia management, delivery planning, and the postnatal period for both mother and baby.

Antenatal care is offered as a package so the cost is known from the beginning, and every visit is with the same obstetrician rather than whoever is on duty.

  • Dating, anomaly and growth scans
  • Haemoglobin and gestational diabetes screening
  • Nutrition and weight-gain guidance
  • Birth plan discussion from the third trimester
  • Postnatal review for mother and baby
  • Lactation support after delivery
What we offer

Maternity Care treatments

Antenatal Packages

Scheduled consultations, scans and bloods across all three trimesters, priced as one package with no surprise additions.

High-Risk Pregnancy

Closer monitoring for hypertension, gestational diabetes, thyroid disease, twins or a previous caesarean, with the physician team alongside.

Postnatal & Newborn

Recovery checks, wound and lactation support, contraception counselling, and the newborn examination and first vaccinations.

What to expect

Care that does not stop at delivery

The six weeks after birth are where most families feel least supported. We keep the postnatal review, lactation help, mood screening and the baby’s first immunisations inside the same clinic, so you are not travelling between three places with a newborn.

  • Postnatal review at one and six weeks
  • Lactation and feeding support
  • Screening for postnatal depression
  • Newborn checks and first vaccinations
Questions

Frequently asked

As soon as you have a positive test. An early dating scan between six and nine weeks confirms viability and gives an accurate due date, which everything else is planned against.

A standard pregnancy involves a dating scan, an anomaly scan at around twenty weeks, and a growth scan in the third trimester. High-risk pregnancies need more, and we will tell you why each one is being ordered.

Antenatal and postnatal care is delivered here, and your obstetrician coordinates the delivery at our partner hospital. Your notes, scans and birth plan travel with you.

The antenatal package covers the listed consultations, scans and routine bloods. If a complication requires additional monitoring we tell you the cost before ordering it.

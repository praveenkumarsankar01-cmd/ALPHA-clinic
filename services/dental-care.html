Dental Care

Routine check-ups, pain relief and full-mouth rehabilitation — with digital imaging on site.

Overview

What is dental care at Alpha Clinic?

Dentistry at Alpha Clinic covers everything from a six-month scale and polish to root canals, crowns and implants. Because the dental chair sits in the same building as our physicians and lab, a patient on blood thinners or with uncontrolled diabetes can be assessed properly before any procedure begins.

Every treatment starts with a digital intraoral X-ray and a written estimate. You will know what is wrong, what it costs and how long it takes before anything is drilled.

  • Digital intraoral and OPG imaging
  • Painless anaesthesia protocol
  • Same-day emergency pain relief
  • Written estimate before treatment
  • Sterilisation audited to NABH standard
  • Child-friendly first-visit appointments
What we offer

Dental Care treatments

Preventive & Hygiene

Scaling, polishing, fluoride application and sealants. A six-month recall keeps small problems from becoming root canals.

Restorative

Tooth-coloured fillings, root canal treatment, post and core, crowns and bridges — matched to your natural shade.

Cosmetic & Aligners

Professional whitening, veneers and clear aligner therapy planned with a digital smile preview before you commit.

What to expect

What your first dental visit looks like

A full-mouth examination, digital X-rays where needed, and a gum-health chart. We then walk you through what we found on screen — not from memory — and give you a printed plan with staged costs. Urgent pain is treated the same visit; elective work is scheduled around you.

  • Full examination and digital imaging
  • Gum health charting and risk assessment
  • On-screen explanation of every finding
  • Printed, staged treatment estimate

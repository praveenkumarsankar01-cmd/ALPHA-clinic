About the clinic

A family practice that grew into a speciality centre

Alpha Clinic opened as a two-room general practice beside Kodambakkam bus stop. Families kept asking for the next thing — a paediatrician, then a gynaecologist, then a dentist — and we built around them rather than sending them across the city.

How it works

Three steps, no runaround

STEP 01

Tell us what's wrong

Book online in under a minute or call the helpline. If you're unsure which department you need, our front desk will place you.
